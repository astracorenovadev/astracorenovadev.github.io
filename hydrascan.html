HydraScan
Available · Android

HydraScan checks the room you are staying in for hidden cameras and unknown tracking tags. A camera the size of a shirt button costs less than dinner. It fits inside a smoke detector, a phone charger, an alarm clock — and a hotel room or a rental is exactly the place where you cannot know what someone left behind. HydraScan turns your phone into five instruments, each watching a different physical channel. The network audit sweeps the Wi-Fi you are on and reads what every device says about itself: open video ports, camera firmware, ONVIF announcements. The Wi-Fi air scan lists every network in range, because a camera does not have to be on yours. The BLE radar hears tracker tags and battery cameras and walks you to them with a hot-and-cold signal. The optics finder catches a lens by its glint under a strobe and sees infrared night-vision LEDs through the front camera. The magnetic detector feels the magnet inside a hidden microphone or speaker. To be worth anything to whoever placed it, a camera has to either transmit or look at you. Every module covers one of those ways — and the room guide shows where to look. Everything runs on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, nothing uploaded. Each module works once for free, so you can see it in action. After that, HydraScan Pro unlocks everything — monthly or yearly.

HydraScan перевіряє кімнату, у якій ви зупинились, на приховані камери й невідомі мітки стеження. Камера завбільшки з ґудзик коштує дешевше за вечерю. Вона вміщується в датчик диму, зарядку, будильник — а номер у готелі чи орендована квартира якраз і є тим місцем, де ви не можете знати, що хтось залишив. HydraScan перетворює телефон на п'ять приладів, і кожен стежить за своїм фізичним каналом. Аудит мережі проходить Wi-Fi, до якого ви підключені, і читає, що кожен пристрій каже про себе: відкриті відеопорти, прошивку камери, оголошення ONVIF. Перепис ефіру Wi-Fi показує всі мережі поблизу, бо камера не зобов'язана бути у вашій. BLE-радар чує мітки стеження й камери на батареях і веде до них шкалою «гаряче — холодно». Пошук оптики ловить об'єктив за відблиском під стробоскопом і бачить інфрачервоне нічне підсвічування через фронтальну камеру. Магнітний детектор відчуває магніт усередині прихованого мікрофона чи динаміка. Щоб бути корисною тому, хто її поставив, камера мусить або передавати, або дивитися на вас. Кожен модуль перекриває один із цих шляхів — а гід по кімнаті підказує, куди дивитися. Усе працює на вашому телефоні. Без облікового запису, без реклами, без аналітики, нічого не вивантажується. Кожен модуль працює один раз безкоштовно — щоб ви побачили його в дії. Далі HydraScan Pro відкриває все: підписка на місяць або на рік.

2. No account, no analytics

There is no sign-up, no profile and no advertising identifier. The app contains no analytics SDK and no crash reporter, so no usage data is generated in the first place.

3. The camera

The optics finder shows a live preview and looks for bright spots in it. Frames are analysed in memory and discarded immediately. Nothing is saved to storage, and nothing is transmitted anywhere.

4. Bluetooth

The radar only listens to advertising packets that nearby devices broadcast publicly. HydraScan never pairs with a device, never connects to one and never sends anything over Bluetooth. On Android 12 and newer it uses the scan permission with the neverForLocation flag, so it cannot derive your location.

5. The network

The audit opens short local connections to addresses inside the network your phone is already on, purely to see which ports answer and what banner they return. No traffic leaves that local network, and no credentials are ever submitted.

6. Wi-Fi and the location permission

The Wi-Fi air scan lists the access points in range, because a camera does not have to be on the network your phone is joined to. Android hands that list out only to apps holding the location permission — the set of networks around you identifies a place almost as precisely as coordinates do. HydraScan asks for it on that one screen and nowhere else, and never reads your position: the app contains no call for coordinates at all. The scan is passive; it reads the beacons access points broadcast publicly and never connects to any of them.

7. What is stored on the device

Your settings, which modules you have already tried, and a short history of your own scans — date, network name, how many devices were found and which were flagged. Settings are part of your ordinary Android backup, so reinstalling the app does not reset them; the scan history is excluded from backup on purpose and can be erased at any time in Settings.

8. Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. HydraScan never sees your card, name or address; it only asks Google whether a purchase is active on this device.

9. Children

HydraScan is a security utility intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app and the date above will change. Questions go to astra.core.nova.dev@gmail.com.
