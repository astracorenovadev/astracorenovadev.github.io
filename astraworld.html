AstraWorld
Available · Android

AstraWorld is a role-playing game played with your feet. Your city is already full of stories. The café on the corner, the park behind your building, the old train station — we just don't see what they could be. AstraWorld shows you. Pick a world — mystery, fantasy, noir, cyberpunk, or one of eleven others — and the map of your city puts on its colors. Real places nearby become points of power, each growing its own legend with every visit. But you have to actually get there. The game won't take your word for it: until GPS brings you to the spot, the quest won't open. And when you arrive, photograph what you see. It's from your exact shot that the Game Master builds the adventure: from this wall, this fountain, this corner. No one else gets the same quest, because no one else has your photo. Then come three steps: tell, shoot, prove. And here the game gets serious. The judge is strict. Photograph the wrong thing — lose health. Too slow — lose a life. Stuck — ask your companion for a hint, but there are only three. Form guilds, trade on the marketplace, build your own saga of locations that remembers every visit. AstraWorld doesn't invent a world for you. It shows you the one you already live in.

4. Generative artificial intelligence

Quests, place descriptions and the Game Master's lines are generated by artificial intelligence — Google's Gemini model, via Google Vertex AI.

Your photos and text are sent to that model to generate game content. This processing runs on Google's global Vertex AI infrastructure and may take place outside the EEA, including in the United States — see section 6 below for details.

Under Google Cloud's terms, customer data is not used to train Google's models.

We apply our own safety constraints when instructing the model, so that it does not propose dangerous, unlawful or offensive actions. No system is perfect: generated content may be inaccurate or unexpected. It is fictional game narrative and is not advice — medical, legal, financial or otherwise.

The final judgement is always yours. Never do anything dangerous or unsafe, even if the game suggests it. If you encounter offensive or unsafe generated content, report it using the in-app report button or by email.

6. Where data is processed — international transfers

Our own servers stay in the EU. The backend we operate ourselves — Cloud Functions and Firestore, including your account, game data and progress backup — runs in the European Union (europe-west1, Belgium), as before.

AI processing is now global. To generate your quests, your photos and text are sent to Google's Vertex AI service, which runs on Google's global infrastructure. This means processing may take place outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, rather than being confined to the EU as previously described.

What has not changed: your photos are still never stored on our servers or Google's — they are sent to the model, used to generate your quest, and discarded. Only a copy on your own phone remains.

Where this processing happens outside the EEA, the transfer is covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) that form part of our agreement with Google Cloud — the standard EU-approved mechanism for this kind of international transfer.

Some ancillary Google services (Analytics, Crashlytics, Play services) may likewise process data outside the EEA; where they do, those transfers also rely on Google's SCCs.
