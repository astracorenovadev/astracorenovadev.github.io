The service

Spoilo is an app for keeping track of expiry dates, with optional AI scanning of packages, receipts and fridge photos, and recipe suggestions. By using it you accept these terms and our Privacy Policy.

AI can make mistakes

Dates, names and storage times recognised by AI, and dates estimated from receipts or typical shelf life, may be wrong. Always check the date printed on the product, especially for medicines, baby food and food for people with allergies. Spoilo is a reminder tool, not a food safety, medical or nutritional service. Recipes are suggestions; check the ingredients for allergens and cook food thoroughly.
