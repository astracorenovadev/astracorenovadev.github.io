3. What HydraScan is

A diagnostic instrument. It reports what it can observe from where you are standing: devices on your current network, Wi-Fi access points and Bluetooth transmitters in range, bright reflections and infrared light in the camera view, and changes in the magnetic field. It interprets those observations and explains its reasoning.

4. What it is not

It is not a guarantee. A device that emits nothing, shows no lens and is switched off cannot be found by any phone app, including this one; nor can a camera that is only a client on someone else's closed network. A clean result means nothing was detected — not that nothing is there.

5. Lawful use

Scanning a network you have no right to inspect is illegal in many countries. HydraScan connects only to devices on the network your phone is already joined to; other networks nearby are only listed from the beacons they broadcast publicly and are never connected to. You are responsible for having the right to be on the network you scan. Use the app in your own home, your hotel room, your rental or your workplace — not against other people.

6. Free tries

Each of the five scanning modules — network audit, Wi-Fi air scan, BLE radar, optics finder and magnetic detector — can be used once free of charge. A try lasts until you leave that module's screen; for the network audit it is one full scan. A try counts as used only once the module has actually started working. The room guide is always free.
